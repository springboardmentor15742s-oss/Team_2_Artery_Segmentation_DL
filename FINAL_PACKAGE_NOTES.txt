ARTERY MEDICAL — FINAL PACKAGE NOTES

This package is a local coronary-image research prototype. It contains a
React/Vite frontend, FastAPI/SQLite backend, local image and annotation data,
training/evaluation scripts, saved model artifacts, and Windows launchers.

Implemented research workflows include case management, image upload and
readability checks, 2D segmentation comparison, optional U-Net inference,
research stenosis-candidate localization, visualization/XAI where supported,
and clinician review. The included dataset reports and model checkpoints are
the evidence for the specific experiments that were run.

The package does not establish prospective clinical performance. Severity
classification and calibrated narrowing are unavailable where valid task
labels or physical calibration are absent. Pixel-based candidates, detector
scores, and heatmaps must not be presented as confirmed findings or diagnoses.

For Windows startup commands, see FINAL_REVIEW_README.md. START_APP.bat starts
the services without retraining. START_PIPELINE.bat runs training and held-out
evaluation when explicitly requested.
